## Goals, boundary and factual basis

The [README](https://github.com/psychoinformatics-de/paper-remodnav/blob/34388d3ca78fbd483e022e4e2851be390b7eebf2/README.md) and manuscript describe a paper with data, statistics/figure code and manuscript sources. This goal/kind assumption was not author-confirmed in this isolated study.

The root contains Makefiles, `code/`, `img/`, manuscript text and `container/`; [.gitmodules](https://github.com/psychoinformatics-de/paper-remodnav/blob/34388d3ca78fbd483e022e4e2851be390b7eebf2/.gitmodules) links raw eyegaze, movement labels and REMoDNaV.
